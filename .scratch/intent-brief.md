# Intent Brief — Interactive 3D Solar System

## Original intent

帮我写一个pico空间版的 Interactive 3D Solar System：A glowing sun in the center with 4 or 5 stylized planets orbiting around it at different speeds. Users can click on a planet to focus the camera on it, popping up a sleek UI card with quick facts about that planet.

## Product goal

Deliver an immersive PICO spatial experience where the user can observe a stylized solar system and inspect individual planets.

## Core capabilities

- A glowing sun at the center of the scene.
- Five stylized planets orbiting the sun at different speeds.
- Planet selection by clicking.
- Camera focus on the selected planet.
- A sleek information card showing quick facts about the selected planet.

## End-to-end user flow

1. The user enters the spatial solar-system scene and sees the glowing sun with planets orbiting around it.
2. The user points at and clicks a planet.
3. The camera focuses on that planet and a compact facts card appears.
4. The user can read the quick facts and select another planet to continue exploring.
